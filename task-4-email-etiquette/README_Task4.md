## 📱 Part B – Social Media Do’s & Don’ts

A checklist was created to promote responsible online behavior among students.

### ✅ Do’s
- Maintain a professional profile  
- Think before posting  
- Respect others online  
- Use privacy settings wisely  
- Share authentic information  

### ❌ Don’ts
- Do not post offensive or harmful content  
- Avoid sharing personal/sensitive information  
- Do not engage in cyberbullying  
- Avoid spreading misinformation  
- Do not misuse digital platforms  

📂 The checklist is saved as **social-media-checklist.md** in the project folder.
